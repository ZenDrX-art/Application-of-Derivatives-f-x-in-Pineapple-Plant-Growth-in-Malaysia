🌱 The Bioscience Problem

In Malaysia's intensive agrotech sectors (such as in Johor and Sarawak), modeling the vegetative biomass accumulation of the MD2 Pineapple is critical. Plants do not grow linearly; they follow a sigmoidal path. By calculating the first derivative $f'(x)$, we transition from measuring static weight to tracking the instantaneous Crop Growth Rate (CGR) in grams per month.
